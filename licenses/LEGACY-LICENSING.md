# Universal Bridge Legacy Licensing

## v0.4.0 and Earlier Public Foundation

Earlier revisions of Universal Bridge were publicly distributed under the CC0 1.0 Universal dedication.

Those previously granted rights are not intended to be revoked by the project's later licensing change.

The Git tag:

`v0.4.0-legacy-cc0`

identifies the final repository baseline designated as the legacy CC0 foundation.

## v0.5.0 and Later Development

Development following the licensing transition is proprietary unless an individual file, SDK, schema, interface, example, library, or other component explicitly states that it is available under another licence.

The main Universal Bridge application, native engines, platform implementations, synchronization technology, commercial integrations, and other proprietary components are not open-source merely because interfaces or documentation may be publicly viewable.

## Third-Party Components

Third-party components remain governed by their own licences.

Before public distribution, Universal Bridge will maintain a third-party software inventory and required copyright/licence notices.
